Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026 • Version 1.0

At WEB FORENSICS (accessible from our platform services including Firebase and Cloud infrastructure), the privacy of our users and investigators is one of our core priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected, how it is processed, and our compliance with global data protection standards and Google API Services User Data Policies.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum required information necessary to provide authentication and defensive analysis capabilities:

  • Account Information (Google OAuth & Email Authentication): When you sign in using Google or email credentials, we receive your basic profile identity, including your primary email address, display name, profile avatar URL, and a cryptographically generated Firebase Unique User Identifier (UID). We request only basic identity scopes (openid, email, profile).
  • User-Declared Metadata: During registration, you may optionally provide a birth age declaration for account age verification purposes.
  • Forensic Investigation Target Queries: When you initiate an analysis, our platform records the public website domain or URL you submit (e.g. example.com) to maintain your session history and display previous investigation reports.
  • System Telemetry & Log Data: We log client IP addresses strictly for rate-limiting, denial-of-service defense, and service health monitoring.

Defensive OSINT Notice: WEB FORENSICS queries only publicly accessible internet infrastructure records (such as DNS nameservers, TLS/SSL certificates, public HTTP headers, and public WHOIS registries). We do NOT access private, confidential, or behind-the-firewall user data.

2. How We Use Collected Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate technical purposes:

  • To authenticate your session and verify authorization to view saved investigation histories.
  • To enforce fair usage quotas and prevent automated resource exhaustion or abuse.
  • To provide your personal dashboard with history synchronization across authenticated sessions.

3. Google User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

WEB FORENSICS' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We do not sell Google user data to any third party.
  • We do not use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements.
  • We do not use Google user data to train generalized machine learning or artificial intelligence models.
  • Human review of user data does not occur except with your explicit affirmative consent or as required for critical security audits.

4. Data Storage, Retention & Security

All account records and investigation histories are persisted within Google Cloud Platform / Firebase Firestore infrastructure. Data is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) and encrypted at rest using AES-256 standard encryption. Role-based security rules strictly isolate your saved investigations so that only your authenticated account can access them.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: You can view all saved investigations and account details directly in your dashboard.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update your account profile and display preferences at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You may request complete deletion of your account, authentication tokens, and all associated investigation history records.
